Portable Bible School (P.B.S)

Portable Bible school aims at training grass root church leaders particularly in rural areas and growing urban centers of Africa.

Training leaders helps to provide the much needed pastoral care for the growing of churches. This is important because most churches do not have, nor can they afford, a full time pastor; some churches are under one pastor.

A successful implementation of P.B.S training in any given community will result in a multiplication of leaders and churches. We would like to make every church a "Disciple Factory"

We train Pastors and Church Leaders from different denominations and churches for 8 weeks. It is called portable because it trains people "where they are" and has been taught in classrooms, homes, prisons, refugee camps, hospitals, under trees, and on rooftops. The teachers follow the students where the training takes place.
