Youth's Ministries

It aims to train youth for Evangelism. After the 3 days of training each youth commits himself to reach out to 3 other youth and make them Disciples of Christ.

Each participant commits to reaching out to at least three other youth, guiding them to Christ and helping them grow as disciples. This multiplication model not only strengthens individual faith but also sparks a movement of revival and leadership among the next generation.

YOUTH EVANGELISM TRAINING

equiping young people with effective evangelism skills and strategies.

DISCIPLE MAKING

Empowering youth to become disciple-makers, with each trained youth committing to reach and disciple three others.
