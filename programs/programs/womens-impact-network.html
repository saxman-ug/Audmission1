Women's Impact Network (WIN)

Envisions Christian women, challenged for deeper Discipleship, resolved to undertake dynamic, Spiritual formation with other women, who in turn will become Empowered Disciple-makers.

The Women’s Impact Network envisions Christian women who are stirred to pursue deeper discipleship and spiritual growth. It challenges them to engage in dynamic spiritual formation alongside fellow women in faith, creating a supportive environment for transformation and empowerment. This network aims to equip women to become effective disciple-makers, spreading the love of Christ and impacting their communities.

WOMEN DISCIPLESHIP

Challenging women to grow deeper in their faith and understanding of God's Word through intentional discipleship programs.
