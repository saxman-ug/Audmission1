Inductive Bible Study (I.B.S)

This program is designed to equip students with the skills to prepare expository Bible studies and sermons directly from Scripture. By learning to observe, interpret, and apply God’s Word for themselves, students not only gain deeper biblical understanding but also experience personal transformation and cultivate a strong, abiding relationship with Jesus Christ.

The Inductive Bible Study method emphasizes the importance of personal study and application of Scripture, enabling students to become effective teachers and disciples. This program is ideal for pastors, church leaders, and anyone seeking to deepen their understanding of the Bible and enhance their teaching skills.

EXPOSITORY BIBLE STUDY TRAINING

Learn the art of preparing and delivering expository Bible studies that bring God's Word to life and transform lives.

INDUCTIVE BIBLE STUDY(IBS)

Master the skills of crafting meaningful sermons through deep biblical study and personal reflection.
