I am excited to have you here. Get ready for the amazing holistic discoveries on this website. You are then at the right place where you will have the opportunity of:

  • Serving God and,
  • Supporting local Communities by reducing poverty

What we are not:

We are not interested in politics in all our gatherings. We prioritize the expansion of Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ and show love and compassion to the community as a whole. AUDM does not consecrate leaders and does not give sacraments. We also don't pretend to know more than what church leaders do and we don't want to duplicate the work local churches are correctly doing as well. We are not in the business of competing with our hosts, the church leaders.

What we are:

AUDM comes as a support to legally established churches and longs to educate and empower forgotten communities especially in remote areas, preaching Peace, Reconciliation and Financial Literacy to minimize poverty.

What makes us unique?

  1. We teach and preach the gospel, looking at the well-being of the local communities as well.
  2. We teach Financial literacy to the community, the only tool able to change the mindset of the community.
  3. We bring theology training wherever churches are found with our Portable Bible Schools. We prioritize where local Christian communities have not enough opportunities to access to Bible Schools, where church leaders have limited access to Christian libraries and where Internet is inexistent.
  4. Knowing what they have as barriers, we go out and don't wait them to come towards us. With our mobile portable schools, we adapt our training to their calendar and discuss Bible with lay church members & clergy as well. We use this opportunity to discuss strategies of extending the Gospel to forgotten communities outside there. We equip them with books and other Christian literature when possible.

Strategy in the field:

We involve some local gifted leaders in our teaching curriculum and thus exchange not only experiences but also their talents.

We use books and literature of other ministries that have the same vision and mission like ours, like:

  • Mail-box club

Our Story

Africa United for Discipleship and Missions (AUDM) was born out of a deep conviction to address the spiritual needs of Africa. Established in 2016, our journey began with a small group of passionate believers who recognized the need for effective discipleship and mission work across the continent.

Despite Christianity's long history in Africa, we observed that many communities still struggled with animistic beliefs and lacked proper biblical training. This realization sparked our mission to equip and empower local leaders to spread the Gospel effectively.

Our Mission

To train, educate, and equip grassroots pastors, church and lay leaders in Biblical knowledge, ministry skills, and character development for effective Kingdom expansion across Africa.

We believe that by empowering local leaders with proper biblical training and ministry skills, we can create a lasting impact in communities across the continent.

Our Vision

An African wide discipleship working with churches in training church leaders until every church is a disciple factory. Our vision is to reach atleast 22 African countries by 2035, creating a united body of Christ built up in love, with disciples making disciples from generation to generation.

We envision a continent where every community has access to biblically sound teaching and where the Gospel transforms lives and communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about AUDM's mission, programs, and impact

What is AUDM's main mission?

AUDM trains and equips grassroots pastors and church leaders across Africa in three key areas: Biblical knowledge, ministry skills, and character development. Our goal is to create lasting impact through empowered local leadership.

How does AUDM support local churches?

We provide comprehensive training through our School of Evangelism, mobile Bible School, and specialized programs for children and youth ministry. All programs are designed to be culturally relevant and accessible to both urban and rural churches.

What regions does AUDM serve?

Currently operating in Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Malawi. Our vision is to expand to 30 African countries by 2030, focusing on regions with the greatest need for biblical training and leadership development.

How can I get involved with AUDM?

You can volunteer with our training programs, support through donations, participate in mission trips, or partner with us in ministry. We also welcome prayer partners and resource development assistance.

What makes AUDM's training programs unique?

Our programs combine biblical teaching with practical ministry skills, cultural sensitivity, and modern technology. We focus on hands-on training, mentorship, and sustainable impact through local leadership development.
