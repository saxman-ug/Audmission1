Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about AUDM's mission, programs, and impact

What is AUDM's main mission?

AUDM trains and equips grassroots pastors and church leaders across Africa in three key areas: Biblical knowledge, ministry skills, and character development. Our goal is to create lasting impact through empowered local leadership.

How does AUDM support local churches?

We provide comprehensive training through our School of Evangelism, mobile Bible School, and specialized programs for children and youth ministry. All programs are designed to be culturally relevant and accessible to both urban and rural churches.

What regions does AUDM serve?

Currently operating in Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Malawi. Our vision is to expand to 30 African countries by 2030, focusing on regions with the greatest need for biblical training and leadership development.

How can I get involved with AUDM?

You can volunteer with our training programs, support through donations, participate in mission trips, or partner with us in ministry. We also welcome prayer partners and resource development assistance.

What makes AUDM's training programs unique?

Our programs combine biblical teaching with practical ministry skills, cultural sensitivity, and modern technology. We focus on hands-on training, mentorship, and sustainable impact through local leadership development.
