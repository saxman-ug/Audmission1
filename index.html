Our Corporate Profile

Who We Are

Africa United for Discipleship and Missions is a Christian Non-Government Organization based in Uganda (East Africa). It was officially established in 2016 with a vision to see Christ increasingly formed in people's lives and multiplied in the lives of others from generation to generation.

While Christianity has been in Africa and Uganda for decades with 80% of Uganda's population identifying as Christians, we recognize that our mission is ongoing as many still retain animistic mindsets that militate against the Christian faith.

We believe in the power of prayer, training, and sending forth trained disciples to reach the lost. This is our continuous commitment until Christ returns for His church.

Our Mission

An African wide discipleship working with churches in training church leaders until every church is a disciple factory. Our vision is to train, educate and equip grassroots pastors, church and lay leaders in Biblical knowledge, ministry skills, and character development for effective Kingdom expansion across Africa.

Our Vision

To reach atleast 22 African countries by 2035, creating a united body of Christ built up in love, with disciples making disciples from generation to generation.

Building a Community of Believers

At Africa United for Discipleship and Missions, we believe in the power of fellowship. Coming together as believers strengthens our faith, encourages spiritual growth, and promotes unity in the body of Christ.

Our fellowship programs provide opportunities for spiritual nourishment, meaningful connections, and collaborative ministry. Whether you're looking to grow in your faith, serve others, or connect with like-minded believers, we invite you to join our community.

Regular gatherings include prayer meetings, Bible studies, ministry training sessions, and community outreach events. Sign up for our newsletter to stay informed about upcoming fellowship opportunities.
